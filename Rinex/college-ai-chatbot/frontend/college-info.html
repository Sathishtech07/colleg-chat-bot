College Information & Student Handbook

Everything you need to know about timings, admissions, library, leave rules, and facilities at SIT.

⏱️

College Timings & Office Hours

  • Working Days: Monday to Saturday (Closed 2nd & 4th Saturdays)
  • Campus Timings: 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM
  • Academic Classes: 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM (Lunch Break: 1:00 PM - 1:45 PM)
  • Administrative Office: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
  • Sundays & Public Holidays: Campus closed
📋

Admissions & Eligibility

  • B.Tech Courses: CSE, ECE, Mechanical, Civil, IT
  • Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics (Min. 50% aggregate)
  • Admission Process: State Entrance Rank Counseling & Management Quota
  • Required Documents: 10th & 12th Marksheets, Transfer Certificate, Entrance Scorecard, Aadhaar, Passport Photos
  • Admission Office Contact: +91 98765 43210 / admissions@sit.edu
📚

Central Library Rules & Hours

  • Location: 2nd Floor, Main Academic Block
  • Timings: 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM (Mon-Sat) | Exam Days: Extended to 9:00 PM
  • Book Borrow Limit: 3 books for undergraduates (14-day renewal)
  • Digital Library: 50+ computers with free access to IEEE & ScienceDirect journals
  • Librarian: Mr. V. K. Murthy (library@sit.edu)
📝

Student Leave Application Procedure

  1. Obtain a Leave Application Form from your Department Office or download from the student portal.
  2. Fill in student details, dates of leave, and valid reason (Medical / Personal / Family Event).
  3. Attach supporting documents (e.g., Medical Certificate for medical leave > 2 days).
  4. Get the signature of your Faculty Mentor and HOD.
  5. Submit the approved form to the Department Office at least 1 day in advance.

Campus Facilities & Amenities

  • Cafeteria & Canteen: Hygienic food, snacks & beverages (7:30 AM - 6:00 PM)
  • Hostels: Separate Boys' & Girls' Hostels with Wi-Fi & 24/7 security
  • Sports Complex: Cricket ground, Football field, Basketball court & Indoor games room
  • Medical Center: Resident doctor & ambulance service available on campus
  • Wi-Fi & Labs: 1 Gbps high-speed Wi-Fi across the entire campus
